# Social post preferences

## Voice
- Direct, technical, and practical.
- First person is OK when describing projects I built.
- Avoid hype, empty AI phrasing, and long emoji chains.

## LinkedIn
- Start with the concrete problem or shipped result.
- Explain the architecture or technical lesson briefly.
- End with a useful takeaway or invitation to discuss.

## X
- Prefer one strong post or a short thread.
- Keep it punchy and specific.
- Avoid engagement bait.

## Safety
- Never publish without my explicit approval.
- If Cloudflare/customer/internal details are present, ask me to confirm they are OK to share.
